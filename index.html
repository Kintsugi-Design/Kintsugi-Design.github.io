Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to some of the most common questions about our services, process, and working with Kintsugi Design.

What industries do you specialize in?

We’ve worked with clients in fintech, e-commerce, healthcare, education, and more. Our adaptable process means we can deliver results for any industry.

How do you approach a new project?

We start with a discovery phase to understand your goals, users, and challenges. Then we move into design, development, and iteration—always keeping you involved and informed.
